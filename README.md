Analysis: 
Comparing the three graphs, turnover margin predicts wins the best. The boxes barely overlap with losing teams having a median turnover margin of -1, while winning teams have a median of +1, so winners almost always finish even or ahead in the turnover battle. Yard differential also shows a clear difference with median of -46 yards in losses and +46 in wins, but its boxes overlap more compared to turnover margin, which means a team can outgain its opponent and still lose. Penalty yards show almost no difference. The medians are 46 yards in losses and 45 in wins, and the boxes are nearly identical, so penalty yards do not do well in predicting the outcome of a game. In conclusion, turnover margin is the best statistic for predicting wins, yard differential is a close second, and penalty yards show little to no correlation.
